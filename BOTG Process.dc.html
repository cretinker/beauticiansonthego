What we need
from you.
Four simple essentials already in your home or venue. Everything else arrives in the car.
Open Space
Enough room to set up a table and move around it comfortably. A living room, kitchen, spare bedroom, or conference suite.
A Power Outlet
For hair dryers, daylight ring lights, nail lamps, and hot towel warming units. One accessible outlet per station.
Good Lighting
Natural light is ideal for color matching and makeup. We bring supplemental LED ring lighting if a room is dark.
Access to Water
A sink or bathtub for shampooing, rinsing, and filling pedicure basins.
