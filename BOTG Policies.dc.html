Section 01
Cancellation & Rescheduling Windows
We understand that schedules shift. Because our beauticians are exclusively assigned to your designated time slot and travel route, we request advance notification for any changes:
  • Individual Appointments (1–2 clients): Cancellations or rescheduling requests received at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled start time incur zero cancellation fee. Requests inside 48 hours are subject to a 50% service charge to compensate the specialist. Cancellations within 4 hours or upon specialist arrival are billed at 100%.
  • Group Spa Parties (6+ guests): Requires 7 days notice for full cancellation or major guest count decreases. Minor guest count changes can be accommodated up to 72 hours prior.
  • Weddings & Bridal Parties: Bridal contracts require 30 days written notice for date changes. Deposits are transferable to a new date within 6 months based on team availability.
  • Sudden Illness or Medical Emergencies: We never penalize clients for sudden medical emergencies or in-care hospital admissions. Contact our concierge as soon as possible and we will gladly reschedule your visit.
Section 02
Travel & Location Access Logistics
Our specialists bring heavy hydraulic tables, styling chairs, lighting rigs, and professional kits into your space. To ensure smooth arrival:
  • Gate & Parking Clearance: Please provide gate codes, community name, building number, elevator access instructions, and visitor parking details during booking.
  • Hotels & Luxury Resorts: We service all major Phoenix and Scottsdale resorts. Please register our specialist name with the valet or front desk to facilitate room entry with equipment.
  • Travel Fee Transparency: Travel fees are calculated based on mileage from our Scottsdale central hub and are completely itemized on your approved quote prior to booking. No mileage or travel surcharges are ever added after the visit.
Section 03
Sanitation & Hospital-Grade Hygiene
Your health and safety are our foremost priority. Every beautician operates under stringent health department protocols:
  • Autoclaved Stainless Implements: All cuticle nippers, shears, and metal tools undergo hospital-grade sterilization and are unsealed in front of you.
  • Single-Use Disposable Liners: Pedicure basins, shampoo bowls, and treatment surfaces utilize single-use medical-grade liners that are disposed of immediately after each service.
  • Fresh Linen Protocol: Freshly laundered, high-heat sanitized linens, styling capes, and towels are used for each individual client.
  • Product Allergies: We carry premium organic, hypoallergenic, and fragrance-free formulas. Please disclose any known skin or botanical allergies during intake.
Section 04
In-Care, Senior & Hospital Protocols
For over twenty-five years, Beauticians On The Go has specialized in compassionate mobile salon care for seniors, hospice, and individuals with limited mobility:
  • Bedside & Wheelchair Adaptations: Our specialists are trained in gentle bedside hair washing, chairside dry cuts, wheelchair styling, and gentle manicure therapy.
  • Caregiver Coordination: We work closely with family members, assisted living staff, and hospice nurses to ensure the appointment does not disrupt medical or rest routines.
  • Dementia & Memory Care Patience: Our beauticians work at the client's pace with soothing, calm communication and complete patience.
Section 05
Payments, Invoicing & Gratuity
Straightforward billing with transparent itemization:
  • Payment Methods: We accept all major Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), Zelle, ACH electronic transfer, and corporate checks for B2B accounts.
  • Corporate W-9 & Invoicing: Corporate wellness days and retreat accounts receive detailed itemized invoices with employer W-9 forms supplied upon booking.
  • Gratuity: Gratuity is at the client's sole discretion and can be added directly to the electronic invoice or given to the specialist in cash upon completion.
Section 06
Service Quality & Satisfaction Guarantee
Our business has flourished for twenty-five years because our clients trust our specialists implicitly. If any element of your service does not meet your expectations, notify our office within 48 hours and we will arrange a complimentary touch-up or resolve the issue immediately.
