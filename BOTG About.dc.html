About Our Collective
Since 2001, the salon
has come to you.
One clear conviction held for over twenty-five years: the salon travels, the client does not. Twenty-five licensed beauticians work through our central hub, covering sixteen cities across four states: and most of them are booking the same loyal clients they served five years ago.
Who we serve
Brides in hotel suites and desert venues. Companies hosting an employee wellness afternoon. Bachelorette gatherings in rented vacation homes. Kids birthday celebrations. And families whose mother or father can no longer navigate a busy salon environment.
Hospitals, hospice, retirement communities, assisted living, and rehabilitation centers are on our schedule every week. Many of those clients have had the same beautician for over a decade.
Who performs the work
Licensed specialists with five to twenty-five years behind the chair, continuing advanced education, and trained in strict sanitary protocols. They own their client relationships, which is why the majority of our appointments are recurring standing visits.
We continuously welcome new licensed specialists with five or more years of experience who value autonomy over waiting for walk-ins.
