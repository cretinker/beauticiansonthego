About us
Since 2001, the salon
has come to you.
One idea, held for twenty-five years: the salon travels, the client does not. Twenty-five licensed beauticians work out of the Scottsdale office, covering sixteen cities across four states — and most of them are booking the same clients they had five years ago.
Who we work for
Brides in hotel suites and desert venues. Offices running a spa afternoon. Bachelorette parties in rented houses. Kids' birthdays. And families whose mother or father cannot get to a salon any more.
Hospitals, hospice, retirement communities, assisted living and rehab centers are on the schedule every week. Some of those clients have had the same beautician for a decade.
Who does the work
Licensed, five to fifteen years behind the chair, still taking classes, trained in current sanitary protocols. They own their client relationships, which is why most bookings are standing appointments rather than one-offs.
We are always hiring. Licensed, five years or more, and you would rather set your own schedule than wait for walk-ins.
