Privacy & Residential
Discretion Policy.
Because we provide luxury treatments inside private residences, gated estates, executive boardrooms, and healthcare environments, we uphold strict non-disclosure, security, and data protection standards.
1. Residential & Property Security
Gate codes, property access directions, and private address details provided during booking are stored strictly within our encrypted dispatch system. They are only disclosed to your assigned specialist on the morning of your visit and are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
2. Client Discretion & Non-Disclosure
All specialists adhere to strict confidentiality agreements. We routinely serve high-profile individuals, public figures, executives, and families seeking private in-home care. Photography or social media sharing of client spaces is strictly prohibited unless explicit written permission is granted by the client.
3. In-Care & Medical Health Disclosures
Health notes regarding client mobility, dementia care, surgery recovery, or allergies are used solely to match the ideal specialist and adapt equipment. All in-care information is handled in accordance with professional privacy and care guidelines.
4. Payment Security & Data Protection
All electronic payment transactions are encrypted and processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant financial gateways. We never store raw credit card numbers on local devices or unencrypted servers.
5. Communications & SMS Dispatch
Phone numbers and email addresses are used exclusively for booking confirmations, arrival timing updates, and day-before reminders. We do not engage in aggressive marketing or unsolicited third-party SMS campaigns.
